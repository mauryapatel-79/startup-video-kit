# Video brief

## Product

- **Name:** Startup Video Kit
- **Website:** github.com/mauryapatel-79/startup-video-kit
- **One-line promise:** Founders get a launch video from their coding agent instead of a video editor.
- **Audience:** Solo founders and small SaaS teams who already use Claude Code or Codex.
- **Top 3 features:**
  1. Paste a URL, get a script, voice, captions and sound.
  2. Free local voice by default; ElevenLabs or HeyGen when you want premium.
  3. Renders 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1.
- **Proof:** none yet (new project), so the film shows the real workflow instead.

## Video

- **Type:** launch film (short)
- **Length:** 22 s
- **Aspect ratios:** 16:9
- **Where:** README, X, LinkedIn
- **Call to action:** the GitHub repo URL

## Voice and sound

- **Voice:** free local (Kokoro, `af_heart`)
- **Tone:** developer-plain
- **Language:** English
- **Music:** none (no music source without a HeyGen sign-in; see CREDITS.md)

## Brand

- **Colours:** ground `#0B0D12`, ink `#F4F1EA`, accent `#FF6A3D`
- **Fonts:** Inter, JetBrains Mono (bundled with HyperFrames)
